Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Who we are

Gita & Grit ("we", "us") operates a private content-publishing application that creates original short videos about Hindu mythology, folk tales and motivation, and uploads them to channels and Pages that we own. This policy explains exactly what that application accesses, what it stores, and what it does not do.

This policy applies to the application and to this website. It does not apply to the platforms we publish to — YouTube, Instagram and Facebook each have their own privacy policies governing content after it is uploaded.

2. What data the application accesses

The application uses official platform APIs with scopes we deliberately keep to the minimum needed to publish our own videos:

PlatformScope requestedWhat it allows
YouTube (Data API v3) youtube.upload Upload a video file to the authorised channel. No read access to other channels, comments, subscribers or analytics.
Instagram (Graph API) instagram_content_publish, instagram_basic Publish a Reel to the authorised professional account. Read the account's own ID and username.
Facebook (Graph API) pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement, pages_show_list, publish_video Publish a Reel to a Page we administer, and list the Pages we own so we can select the correct one.

We do not request messaging, advertising, audience-insight, follower-list or comment-moderation permissions.

3. What we store

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens — stored as plain files on the operator's own computer, in a directory readable only by that user. These tokens authenticate our own uploads.
  • Your own video files and generated images — stored locally on the same computer until uploaded.
  • Publication records — a local database noting which video was published to which platform, and the returned post identifier. This exists to prevent accidental duplicate posting.

We operate no server and no database in the cloud. Nothing is transmitted to us, because there is no "us" to transmit to — the application runs entirely on the operator's machine.

4. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect personal data about any viewer, subscriber or third party.
  • We do not access other users' accounts, videos, messages or analytics.
  • We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or third-party analytics on this website.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade or share data with anyone.
  • We do not use platform API data to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not serve advertising.

5. How we use the data

Accessed data is used for exactly two purposes: to authenticate the upload of a video we created, and to record where it was published so we do not publish it twice. No secondary use, profiling or automated decision-making about any person takes place.

6. Legal basis

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on our own consent as the account owner — we authorise the application ourselves and can withdraw that authorisation at any time. No third party's data is processed.

7. Retention and deletion

Tokens are retained until we revoke access or delete them. Local video files are retained until we delete them. Publication records are retained for as long as the channel operates, and can be deleted at any time by removing the local database file. Revoking access in the platform settings immediately stops the application from uploading.

8. Sharing and third parties

Video files are transmitted directly to YouTube and Meta for publication. That is the only outbound transfer of data. Beyond that, we share data with nobody. We do not use sub-processors, and we do not use a hosting provider for personal data because no personal data leaves the operator's computer.

9. Security

Tokens are stored with file permissions restricting access to the operating system user account. The computer is protected by a login password and disk encryption. Tokens are never committed to version control; the repository that contains this website contains no credentials of any kind.

10. Your rights

Because the application processes no third-party personal data, there is no third-party data to access, correct, export or erase. As the account owner you can revoke all access at any time:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Meta: facebook.com/settings?tab=applications

Revoking access takes effect immediately and stops all further uploads.

11. International transfers

We do not transfer data internationally, because we do not operate a server. Uploads go directly from the operator's computer to the platform's own infrastructure, under that platform's terms.

12. Children

The application is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated and the revised policy takes effect when published. Material changes will be reflected here before the application continues to be used.
